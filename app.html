How to read this board

The big percentage is the model’s win probability for the named pick. The small “band” is what picks stated at that level actually won historically.

The colored stripe is the trust rating — how often picks built on this shape of evidence have won, measured per tour:

Dashed stripes are shown but not counted — below the validated 60% confidence floor. Tags like PPC or FORM-OPPOSED are measured cautions: they explain how a miss would most likely happen, they never change the number.
