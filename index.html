How it works

Measure the players.Ratings, form, surface history, opponent quality, fatigue and 60+ other factors — computed strictly from what was knowable before the match. No hindsight, ever.
Predict twice, independently.A machine-learned engine and a human-readable scorecard each pick a winner. When they agree with the rankings, confidence is highest; when they split, the page says so.
Grade the pick, then keep score in public.The trust color states the historically measured win rate for picks with that exact evidence shape — and every day's results are tallied against it, live on this page.
TPE is a private research project. All figures are model estimates measured against historical and live results; they are not betting advice, and no wagering service is offered. Trust-band percentages are walk-forward measurements on 2024-present data and are re-verified weekly. Result integrity: the match outcomes underlying TPE's validation era are cross-verified against an independent commercial data source at 99.93% winner agreement (~17,500 matches, 2024–25), with roughly a third of 2025 results independently confirmed.
