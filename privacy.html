ServeVolley Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026 · applies to servevolley.com and the ServeVolley apps for iOS and Android

ServeVolley is operated by BXA LLC, Silver Spring, Maryland, United States ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as the service needs, we run no advertising or analytics trackers, and we never sell personal data.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no cross-app or cross-site tracking.
  • No third-party analytics software in the app or website.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data for marketing.
  • No marketing email. The only email we send is the sign-in code you request and essential service notices.

Who processes data for us

We use these providers, each only for the purpose listed. Each has its own privacy policy and handles data under contract with us.

  • Stripe — payments for web subscriptions. Stripe receives your email and payment details directly; we receive only the status and a customer id.
  • Apple — App Store purchases and, if enabled, push notifications on iOS.
  • Google — Google Play purchases and, if enabled, push notifications on Android.
  • Resend — delivers sign-in code emails.
  • Hetzner Online — hosts our servers (United States data center).
  • GitHub — hosts the static website pages.

Where data is stored

Our servers are in the United States. If you use ServeVolley from outside the United States, including the European Union or United Kingdom, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States under this policy.

How long we keep it

  • Account data (email, trial and subscription status) for as long as your account exists.
  • Request logs for up to 30 days.
  • Subscription and payment records for as long as tax and accounting law requires, typically seven years, in anonymised form where possible.

Your choices and rights

  • Sign out from the Account sheet in the app at any time.
  • Notifications can be turned off in your device settings.
  • Delete your account by emailing support@servevolley.com from the address on the account. We delete the account and its data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law. Deleting the account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription; cancel that in your store account first.
  • You may ask us for a copy of the data we hold about you, or to correct it, at the same address.
  • Residents of the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, California and other regions with privacy laws have the rights those laws grant, including access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction and objection, and the right to complain to your local supervisory authority. We rely on performance of our contract with you (providing the service) and our legitimate interest in security as the legal bases for processing.

Children

ServeVolley is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in codes are stored hashed, sessions can be revoked, and payment details never touch our servers. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will tell you as the law requires.

Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will post the new version here with a new date and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
