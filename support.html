ServeVolley Support

Help with the app, your account and your subscription
ContactEmail support@servevolley.com. Write from the email address on your account so we can find it. We answer within two business days.
Signing inTap Start free trial or Sign in, enter your email, and type the 6-digit code we send. The code lasts 15 minutes. There is no password. If the code does not arrive, check spam for a message from sign-in@servevolley.com and wait a minute before requesting another; there is a limit of five codes per hour.
Free trialEvery new account gets 14 days of ServeVolley Premium from first sign-in, with no payment method. The Account sheet shows how many days are left.
SubscribingWhen the trial ends, tap Subscribe. In the iOS app the purchase goes through the App Store; in the Android app through Google Play; on the website through Stripe. One subscription unlocks the same account on every platform, so sign in with the same email everywhere.
Already subscribed but the app shows the paywall?Open Account and tap Refresh. If you subscribed through the App Store or Google Play on another device or after reinstalling, tap Restore purchases in the Account sheet while signed in with your account email.
CancellingApp Store: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ServeVolley. Google Play: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Web: Account → Manage billing. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access to the end of the paid period. Refunds for store purchases are handled by Apple or Google.
Deleting your accountEmail us from the account address and ask for deletion. We remove the account and its data within 30 days. Cancel any store subscription first, because deleting the account does not stop store billing.
NotificationsThe app can tell you when the day's board is ready and when a pick settles. Turn them on when asked, or later in your device settings. They can be switched off there at any time.
Reading the predictionsThe Guide tab in the app explains the percentages, the trust colours and the cautions. Predictions are statistical estimates for research and education, not betting advice.
