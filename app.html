How to read this app

The big percentage is the model’s win probability for the named pick. The small “history” number is what picks stated at that level actually won historically.

The colored stripe is the trust rating — how often picks built on this shape of evidence have won, measured per tour:

A dashed stripe marks a prediction below the model’s 60% win-probability threshold. These matches are shown but excluded from the selective record. The threshold was chosen by historical coverage-versus-accuracy testing; it is not a 60% historical trust rating. Tags like PPC or FORM-OPPOSED are measured cautions: they explain how a miss would most likely happen; they never change the number.

Tap any match for the full anatomy: evidence, cautions, and side-by-side player stats.
